The *Taipei Times* is Taiwan's leading English-language daily newspaper, regularly publishing analyses from international experts.

I'm publishing an op-ed today on the gap between Taiwan's AI ambitions and its natural resource constraints.

The island wants to become a global AI powerhouse, with ten major infrastructure projects. But it imports 97% of its energy and only has ten days of LNG reserves. TSMC already consumes nearly 10% of national electricity — potentially 24% by 2030.

Water is the other blind spot. TSMC absorbs 150,000 tons of water per day, and AI data centers are adding to this pressure. During recent droughts, 74,000 hectares of rice paddies were sacrificed to maintain industrial supply.

I advocate for transparent governance of trade-offs, geothermal development, and binding regulations on data center water consumption.
