I proposed in **Rue89 Lyon**, on the occasion of a government reshuffle, the creation of a proper Ministry of Digital Affairs.

The article argues for consolidating competencies scattered across several ministries — digital economy, e-government, telecoms, open data — into a coherent structure with real cross-ministerial authority, commensurate with the stakes of the state's digital transformation.
