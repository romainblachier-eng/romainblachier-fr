I published an op-ed in **Le HuffPost** on the siege of Kobané by the Islamic State and the paralysis of the international community.

The article examines the contradictions of Turkish policy, the hesitations of the coalition, and the fate of the Syrian Kurds left facing ISIS. It argues for a more coherent Western engagement, capable of preventing another city from falling under jihadist control.
