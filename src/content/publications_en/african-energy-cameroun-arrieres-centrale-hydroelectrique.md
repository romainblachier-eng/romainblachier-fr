## African Energy

**African Energy** is a UK-based publication covering energy in Africa, combining news, data and consultancy. I am its [Central Africa correspondent](https://www.africa-energy.com/about-african-energy/staff-romain-blachier).

## The article

Unpaid bills from Cameroon's state-owned power buyer piled up at a major hydroelectric plant until its guarantee was nearly used up. The plant's finances should recover once new high-voltage infrastructure enters service.
