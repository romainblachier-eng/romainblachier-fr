## African Energy

**African Energy** is a UK-based publication covering energy in Africa, combining news, data and consultancy. I am its [Central Africa correspondent](https://www.africa-energy.com/about-african-energy/staff-romain-blachier).

## The article

A large hydroelectric power plant in Cameroon saw significant arrears built up by the state-owned offtaker, almost exhausting its guarantee. Its fiscal position should improve once new high-voltage infrastructure is commissioned.
