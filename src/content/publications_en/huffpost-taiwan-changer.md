I published an op-ed in **Le HuffPost** on the dynamics of transformation unfolding in Taiwan.

The island stands at the intersection of several simultaneous shifts: internal political change, a reconfiguration of its economic ties with mainland China, and the rising influence of its civil society. These converging forces are shaping a profoundly changing Taiwan — both in its identity and its geopolitical positioning.
