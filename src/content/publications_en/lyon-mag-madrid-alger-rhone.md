I published an op-ed in **Lyon Mag** on the climate transformation of the Rhône valley.

The evidence accumulates: summer temperatures, river flow levels, spontaneous vegetation — the region increasingly resembles what Madrid or Algiers experience. The article builds on research I began in 2017 on Lyon's climate trajectory and asks what concrete adaptation measures are needed: urban planning, water management, agriculture, and territorial climate governance.
