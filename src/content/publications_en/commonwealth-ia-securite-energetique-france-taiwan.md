I have an op-ed in **[CommonWealth Magazine](https://english.cw.com.tw/article/article.action?id=5005)** (天下雜誌), in its *Insight* section, published on 16 September 2026: "How France and Taiwan Can Make AI Investment Strengthen Energy Security".

It follows on from the 28th Taiwan-France Economic Cooperation Conference, held in Paris on 10 September 2026 at the Cercle de l'Union interalliée at the initiative of CIECA and MEDEF International. I spoke there on energy security and geopolitics.

The op-ed asks how Taiwan's artificial intelligence boom can reinforce the power system rather than put it under strain. Grid access is becoming the bottleneck: capacity held by a data centre may be missing for a factory that wants to electrify, and AI that drives up gas consumption deepens the island's dependence on cargoes arriving by sea.

I propose trading land, incentives and grid connection for commitments from the developer: paying a share of the substations and lines the project requires, keeping to a firm consumption schedule, installing storage or flexibility on site. I also suggest a French-Taiwanese project in one industrial zone, to measure what storage and flexible consumption preserve during an outage and to publish the results.
