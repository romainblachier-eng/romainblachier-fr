I published an analysis in **Lyon Mag** on climate change at the Lyon scale.

At a time when snow in the city has become an event — prompting everyone to reach for their phones before it melts — the article asks a serious question: what will Lyon's winters look like in a few decades? By connecting global IPCC data to observable local indicators, it traces the trajectory of Lyon's climate and anchors the reality of change in everyday life.
