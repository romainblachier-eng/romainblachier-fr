With Jon Marks, I co-wrote an analysis in **[African Energy](https://www.africa-energy.com/news-centre/article/eni-fronts-republic-congo-grid-repair-bill-distribution-lease)** (issue 550, 4 November 2026) titled "Eni fronts Republic of Congo grid repair bill as distribution lease stays frozen".

**African Energy** is a UK-based publication covering energy in Africa, combining news, data and consultancy. I am its [Central Africa correspondent](https://www.africa-energy.com/about-african-energy/staff-romain-blachier).

The piece reviews the power sector in the Republic of Congo. Eni is advancing the money to rehabilitate the high-voltage line between Pointe-Noire and Brazzaville, which is essential to the grid; the work should finish soon and ought to mean fewer blackouts in the capital. Other reforms are moving more slowly than the World Bank would like: the distribution lease is at a standstill, and part of the funding the Bank designed to bring in the private sector is still blocked.
