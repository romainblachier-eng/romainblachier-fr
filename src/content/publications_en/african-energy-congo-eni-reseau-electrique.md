## African Energy

**African Energy** is a UK-based publication covering energy in Africa, combining news, data and consultancy. I am its Central Africa correspondent.

## The article

Eni expects to soon complete the refurbishment of the vital Pointe-Noire to Brazzaville high-voltage transmission line, which should bring relief to power outages in the capital. The World Bank is pressing for faster progress on other sector reforms, while a tranche of its innovative private sector structure financing remains blocked.
