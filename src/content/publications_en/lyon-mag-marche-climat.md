I published an op-ed in **Lyon Mag** in the wake of the large climate march in Lyon, arguing that street mobilisation must translate into concrete policy instruments.

The article sets out the levers available: carbon pricing, building energy renovation, public transport investment, and territorial climate governance — and calls on citizens not to let the momentum dissipate without institutional translation.
