I have an op-ed in English in **[Domino Theory](https://dominotheory.com/mbappe-affair-augurs-poorly-for-free-speech-in-europe/)**, "Mbappe Affair Augurs Poorly for Free Speech in Europe", published on 10 November 2026 in the Opinion section.

**Domino Theory**, an English-language online outlet based in Taiwan, covers East Asian geopolitics, notably cross-strait relations; its editor and co-founder, Ron Brownlow, has written for the Taipei Times, Barron's, the New York Times and Newsweek.

For laughing at a meme that showed him as Mao, Kylian Mbappé lost three Chinese partners, and Chinese television dropped La Liga. I criticise Europe's response: apologies from La Liga and from the player, silence from the French Football Federation, UEFA, FIFA and almost every French politician. Yet since China's pressure on Lithuania, Europe has had an anti-coercion instrument. I call on UEFA and the EU to say that going after companies or players for a political opinion concerns all of Europe and its freedom of expression. Keeping quiet over a football affair weakens Europe's voice in advance on weightier matters, Taiwan included.
