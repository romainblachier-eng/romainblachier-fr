I argued in **Lyon Mag** for the return of night trains departing from Lyon.

Decarbonised travel, useful journey time, competitiveness with aviation, and better connectivity for underserved regions: the economic and climate case is strong. The article explains why Lyon should position itself as a hub for European night trains, moving beyond the purely high-speed logic that has dominated French rail policy for thirty years.
