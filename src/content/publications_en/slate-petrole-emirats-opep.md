## The argument: the real reason behind the UAE's departure from OPEC

The press interpreted the UAE's withdrawal from OPEC as a reaction to the Iran-Hormuz war. In this article, I show that this reading is misleading: the departure is the culmination of a fifteen-year strategy, set in motion well before the Hormuz crisis, aimed at maximising oil rent before hydrocarbon reserves run dry.
