I published an op-ed in **[Journal du Cameroun](https://fr.journalducameroun.com/socadel-cinq-mois-apres-de-bons-chiffres-et-une-proposition-avant-la-saison-seche/)** on the first five months of Socadel, the operator in charge of electricity distribution in Cameroon.

## In brief

- **Measurable results.** Between April and August, outage alerts fell by **70%**. Coverage of the North network rose from about 70% to over 95%, and the South network now stands at 98%. Socadel makes **11,000 new connections a month** and serves more than 2.4 million households.
- **A financial balance that is still fragile.** Producing a kilowatt-hour costs **118 CFA francs**, against an average of 85 francs paid by customers; household tariffs have not moved since 2012, and the State must compensate 79 billion CFA francs of tariff subsidies for 2025.
- **My proposal.** Publish the average length of outages by region every month, so progress can be tracked transparently before the dry season of late 2026 and early 2027.
