I published an article in **Libération** on the fate of Middle Eastern Christians, facing unprecedented persecution in the wake of jihadist advances across Iraq and Syria.

The piece examines whether these ancient communities — Chaldeans, Syriacs, Copts, Maronites — can survive the regional upheaval, and what responsibilities the international community bears in the face of what amounts to a silent religious cleansing.
