I published an op-ed in **Le HuffPost** for World Toilet Day, to remind readers that behind a seemingly unusual title lies a major development challenge.

One in three people worldwide has no access to adequate sanitation. This sanitation deficit is a direct cause of child mortality, preventable disease, gender inequality and school exclusion. The article addresses public health, infrastructure, international cooperation and human rights — more than enough reason to take the subject seriously.
