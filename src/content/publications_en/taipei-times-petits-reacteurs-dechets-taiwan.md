I have a feature in the **[Taipei Times](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/feat/archives/2026/11/18/2003867929)**, published on 18 November 2026 on page 12 of the Features section: “Taiwan’s small-reactor plans face an unresolved waste-disposal problem”.

Taiwan is weighing small modular reactors and microreactors while it still has no permanent site for its nuclear waste. The piece starts from National Tsing Hua University’s plan to swap its research reactor for a US-designed microreactor; under present US policy, the developer will not take the used fuel back. It then turns to two blind spots in the law: the Electricity Act keeps nuclear power for majority state-owned companies, and no rule says whether a private operator paying into the back-end fund could pass its waste on to the state.

The feature draws on academics at Tsing Hua and National Taiwan University, an analyst at the Institute for National Defense and Security Research, and the Green Citizens’ Action Alliance, which rejects these reactors.
